## 🚀 About Me

<div align="center">

I am a developer and DevOps engineer based in Sabzevar, Iran. I specialize in building serverless infrastructure, custom network tunneling, and blazing-fast desktop/web applications. 

<br>

| ⚡ What I Do | 🎯 Focus Areas |
|-------------|----------------|

| 💻 **App Development:** Crafting native Rust/Tauri desktop browsers | 🎮 **Game Dev:** Writing custom Java/Forge Minecraft PvP clients |
