> Breaking digital borders. One connection at a time.

[Website](https://oryvex.top) • [Telegram](https://t.me/oryvex_vpn) • [Discord](https://discord.gg/oryvex)

**OryvexVPN** is a free, secure, and reliable VPN client built by privacy advocates. Our mission is to make the internet accessible for everyone—without tracking, without logs, and without limits.

## ✨ Features
* ⚡ **Smart Routing:** Auto-connects via WireGuard, MASQUE, MASQUE², or Gool.
* 🧅 **Built-in Tor:** Optional Tor routing with bridge support.
* 🦊 **Fox Mode:** Route through Mozilla's Firefox VPN service.
* 🛡️ **Zero Logs:** We never store or track your browsing history.
* ♾️ **Unlimited:** No data caps or time limits.
* 🧩 **Obfuscation:** HTTP/2, HTTP/3, and TLS fragmentation to bypass restrictions.
